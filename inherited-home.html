06Guidance

What this home is asking the new owner to handle first.

Drawn from the realities of an inherited record — not generic homeowner advice. Three working items for an inherited home, right now.

Photograph the systems while you’re here
Data plates on the HVAC, water heater, and electrical panel encode install dates, model numbers, and warranty references. A few photographs today become the spine of the systems record.
Builds the systems record
Pull the permit history from the county
Greenville County, like most counties, retains building permits for 30 years or more. One request typically returns roof, HVAC, electrical, and addition permits going back decades.
Closes early gaps in the timeline
Ask the previous owner what they remember
One conversation often returns the contractor who replaced the roof, the technician who maintained the HVAC, and the year the kitchen was remodeled. Memory is a vault when the paper trail isn’t.
A single call becomes a record
07Transfer-ready record

An inherited home arrived without a record. The one you begin today is what the next owner will inherit instead — and what the property will carry forward, intact.
