Where the proof lives.

The Vault keeps proof connected to the home.

Kodu keeps important property documents, photos, warranties, receipts, inspections, and project records attached to the home they belong to, so the homeowner can find the proof when it matters.

01Proof in context

How one document becomes useful.

Kodu helps the homeowner know where the proof lives before something breaks, gets questioned, or needs to transfer.

1

Uploaded record

A receipt, warranty, inspection, manual, note, or photo is captured as proof inside the private record.

2

Attached to the home

The record connects to the system, project, room, area, or property detail it helps explain.

3

Appears in the timeline

When the proof belongs to work that happened, it can support the property memory as an event.

4

Supports what comes next

The same proof can inform guidance, future service, project history, insurance questions, or transfer.

02Record organization

Vault categories are ways proof stays connected.

These are not page tabs pretending to be the app. They are the organizing ideas a homeowner can use to understand where evidence belongs in the property memory.

Interior

Rooms and inside work

Paint records, fixture receipts, appliance notes, and interior project history.

Exterior

Outside systems

Roof, siding, gutters, windows, drainage, doors, decks, and exterior repairs.

Utility

Core home systems

HVAC, electrical, plumbing, water supply, gas, security, and related service proof.

Projects

What changed

Before and after proof, budgets, decisions, permits, invoices, and completion notes.

Documents

Important records

Manuals, inspections, permits, disclosures, and other records that explain the home.

Photos

Visual proof

Condition photos, progress photos, issue photos, and evidence tied to time and place.

Insurance / warranty

Coverage proof

Policies, warranties, claim-related details, and proof a homeowner may need later.
