SYSTEMS LAYER

Know what your home depends on.

Kodu helps homeowners keep a private record of the systems that keep the property working, from install dates and service history to warranties, receipts, manuals, and replacement context.

Systems are not just equipment. They are part of the property’s living record, tied to maintenance history, proof, project history, and future decisions across the full property.

01System record

What a system record keeps.

A system record helps a homeowner understand what exists, when it changed, where the proof lives, and what may matter next.

1

Install date

The install date gives age and replacement context, especially when a system begins to need more attention.

2

Service history

Service history keeps maintenance from disappearing across visits, seasons, and household memory.

3

Warranty

A warranty is more useful when it stays close to the system it protects and the date it may expire.

4

Manuals

Manuals keep care instructions, model details, and maintenance requirements close to the equipment.

5

Proof and photos

Receipts, photos, notes, and records help explain what happened and where the proof lives.
