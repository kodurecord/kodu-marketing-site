Home records

What records should every homeowner keep?

A complete answer, organized as the seven layers of a property’s record — the same structure Kodu uses to organize every home. Each layer below corresponds to a section that exists in the product.

02Blueprint

Document the physical home.

Floor plan. Survey. Permits. Photographs of the inside of walls before drywall went back up. Locations of shutoffs. The records that describe what is here, and where.

03Vault

Keep the evidence behind every claim.

Warranties. Closing documents. Inspection reports. Receipts for major work. Model and serial numbers. Contractor names with license numbers. The records that give every claim about the home its standing.
