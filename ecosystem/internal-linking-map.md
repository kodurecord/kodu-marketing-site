# Internal Linking Map — Batch 1

How the 10 Batch 1 pages link to each other, and how every page funnels toward the sample property record.

---

## Linking principles

Three rules govern every internal link in Batch 1:

1. **Every page links to the sample property record.** Not as an aside — as the natural endpoint of the page's argument. The sample record is the conversion engine; pages exist to lead there calmly.
2. **Every page belongs to a cluster of four related pages.** The "Continue reading" block at the bottom of each page surfaces four neighbors, chosen for emotional and topical proximity, not arbitrary cross-linking.
3. **No more than one link per ~150 words of body copy.** Inline links inside body paragraphs are restrained. Most cross-linking happens at the page's edges (related block, continuity card, sample-bridge).

---

## The structural nodes

Three pages carry disproportionate weight in the link graph.

### Entry node — `/what-home-records-should-you-keep` (Page 1)

The strongest SEO + emotional entry point. Linked-to from every other Batch 1 page. Establishes the four-part Kodu model (blueprint, vault, timeline, guidance) that every other page references.

**Inbound links from:** all 9 other pages
**Outbound links to:** Pages 2, 3, 8, 10 (related); sample-property-record; request-access

### Philosophical foundation — `/why-homeowners-lose-property-history` (Page 10)

The page that names the structural problem. Acts as the "deep read" for anyone who's converted on the emotional level and wants the why. Heavily linked-to from emotional-trigger pages.

**Inbound links from:** Pages 1, 2, 6, 7 (related)
**Outbound links to:** Pages 7, 1, 6, 2 (related); sample-property-record; request-access

### Conversion bridge — sample-property-record (Batch 2)

Linked from every single Batch 1 page in three places: the continuity card, the sample-bridge component, and the navigation. The page does not exist yet (Batch 2) but every link is in place for it.

**Inbound links from:** all 10 pages, three times each
**Outbound links:** (defined in Batch 2)

## Navigation links (every page)

The top navigation on every Batch 1 page exposes the same five destinations:

1. Overview (homepage)
2. Sample Record (sample-property-record.html)
3. Homeowners (organizing-home-records.html — the most useful homeowner anchor)
4. Systems (roof-records.html — the most-searched system anchor)
5. Insurance (home-insurance-documentation.html — the only insurance page in Batch 1)

These are stable across batches. New batches add categories (Transfer, Guidance) but do not remove these.

## Footer links (every page)

The footer adds depth without competing with the body links:

- Product: Overview, Sample record, Start your record
- Homeowners: New homeowners, Inherited a home, Preparing to sell, Organizing records
- Systems: Roof, HVAC, Appliances
- Company: About, Security, Contact

Several footer links currently point to pages that don't exist yet (`new-homeowners.html`, About, Security, Contact). These resolve to 404 in the Batch 1 deploy and are filled in as later batches ship. This is intentional — the footer is the long-term skeleton, and shipping it complete signals the ecosystem's full scope.
